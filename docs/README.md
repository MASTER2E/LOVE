# 🐀 All About Rats

Rats are smart, social rodents that get a worse reputation than they deserve.

## Quick Facts
- **Smart:** they solve mazes, learn their names, and can be litter-trained
- **Social:** they live in colonies and get genuinely depressed when kept alone
- **Ticklish:** they emit ultrasonic "laughter" chirps when tickled
- **Clean:** they groom themselves more often than cats do
- **Empathetic:** lab rats will free a trapped cage-mate before grabbing food

## Why It Matters
Rats are the backbone of behavioral and medical research, and giant African
pouched rats are trained to sniff out landmines and tuberculosis. Not bad for
an animal most people run from.
